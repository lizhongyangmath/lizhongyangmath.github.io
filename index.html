I am a tenured Associate Professor of Mathematics at the University of Connecticut.

My research lies at the interface of probability, combinatorics, and statistical mechanics, with emphasis on percolation, random tilings, dimer models, limit shapes, and critical phenomena.

Research

  • Percolation and probability on graphs
  • Dimer models, random tilings, and limit shapes
  • Integrable probability and algebraic combinatorics
